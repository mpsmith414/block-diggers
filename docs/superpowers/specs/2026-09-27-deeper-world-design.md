# Block Diggers — The Deeper World (Dinos, Bricks and Space) — Design

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Status:** Approved by the owner ("let's do it"; he loves dinosaurs, Lego-style bricks and space). Built without further input.
**Builds on:** the main design, the expansion design, and everything in them (cozy feel, no text a child needs to read, gentle hazards).

## Why

After 39 minutes the player had almost everything unlocked and asked for **deeper levels**. This update adds four new layers under the crystal caverns, new treasure and tools to reach them, three new buildings ending in a **rocket to the Moon**, dinosaur pets, and two requests from the player himself: a **lava monster** and **drinking the water**. Costs are set so this content lasts many sessions, not one.

## 1. Four new layers (the mine grows to 390 rows)

| # | Layer | Rows | Host rock | Dig it with | Ores | Creature (gentle) | Something special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Dino Bone Beds** | 189–238 | sandstone (tan, fossil flecks) | diamond pick | **amber**, gold, diamond | **pterodactyls** (fly like bats) | **dino eggs**, giant dinosaur skeletons in the cave walls, amber with bugs in it, oasis pools |
| 6 | **Brick Caverns** | 239–288 | toy bricks with studs, in red, blue, yellow and green | amber pick | **bricks**, emerald, amber | **wind-up toy robots** (walk like slimes; jump on them) | **springy bounce blocks**, rainbow brick patterns |
| 7 | **Meteor Field** | 289–338 | dark space rock full of tiny stars | brick drill | **star shards**, diamond, bricks | **little aliens** that float (bump = bonk) | **low gravity** (big floaty jumps), **meteorites** (crack open like geodes, full of star shards) |
| 8 | **The Core** | 339–388 | warm glowing core rock | star drill | star shards, every gem | **ember wisps** (float like bats) | the **Heart of the World**: a giant 3×3 gem at the very bottom, lava pools (lava-monster heaven) |

Row 389 is the bedrock floor.

- **Discovery:** reaching a layer for the first time shows a big banner (the layer's icon, confetti, a fanfare) and earns a **layer badge** sticker.
- **Depth meter:** the new layers are added in their colours, and your deepest-ever row is marked with a small flag.

## 4. The elevator

The **Minecart** can now take you to the top of **any layer you have already reached**. Pressing A at the cart opens a picker of layer icons, and locked layers show a padlock. You arrive in a lit station with rails.

## 5. Camp: three new plots and buildings (the camp grows to 108 cells)

The new plots are at cells 86, 93 and 100, past the decorating meadow, which stays where it is.

| Building | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Dino Park** | 20 amber + 10 gold | A fenced park with palm trees where your **baby dinosaurs** live. Every trip home they dig up a present: **+2 amber**. |
| **Toy Workshop** | 30 brick + 10 gold | Unlocks **brick decorations** at the stall: brick castle, brick car, rainbow arch, brick robot. A little wind-up robot toddles around camp. |
| **Rocket Ship** | 40 brick + 20 star + 1 Heart | **Fly to the Moon!** Press A at the rocket for a countdown, lift-off and a trip to a whole new place to dig (§7). |

## 6. Dinosaur pets

**Dino eggs** (big and speckled) are found in the Dino Bone Beds: 2 per mine, of the kinds you don't have yet. They hatch in the nest like other eggs, and live in the Dino Park if it's built. Like the other pets, they come on every trip.

| Pet | Helps by |
|---|---|
| **Baby T-rex** | Every 8 s it gives a tiny **ROAR**, and nearby slimes, bats and critters within 5 blocks poof away. |
| **Baby Triceratops** | A **boulder buddy**: it counts as a second pusher, so boulders move even when you're playing alone. It also headbutts boom blocks to light them. |

## 7. The Moon

- **Getting there:** press A at the built Rocket. There's a 3-2-1 countdown (big number icons), smoke and flames, and a lift-off where the camera follows the rocket up past the clouds into space. The Earth shrinks behind you, and you land on the Moon.
- **The Moon mine:**
  - a grey cratered surface with the Earth hanging in the black sky;
  - low gravity everywhere;
  - moon rock (grey craters, rows 1–60), then glowing **space crystal caves** (rows 61–100);
  - it uses the same engine as the mine, with 102 rows.
- **Moon treasure:** star shards, diamond and emerald, plus **moon cheese**, a joke ore (yellow, holey) worth nothing but hilarious to collect. It gets its own sticker.
- **Moon creatures:** **moon blobs**, soft squishy aliens (jump on them).
- **First visit:** you plant a **flag** at the landing spot (a sticker). Hold B to be **beamed back** to the rocket, with a teleport sparkle instead of a rope.
- **Pages:** the Space sticker page is all Moon things.

## 8. The player's own requests

### Lava Monster

- Touching lava (anywhere, including the deep layer and the Core) **no longer bonks you**. Instead you **turn into a Lava Monster** for **8 seconds**.
- **The look:** your character glows orange-red with dark rocky patches, drops embers, and has bright yellow eyes. There's a big *roar* sound.
- **While you're a lava monster:**
  - lava doesn't hurt: walk and swim through lava pools;
  - you **dig 2× faster**;
  - you glow (extra light);
  - creatures that touch you poof away instead of bonking you.
- **The end:** you turn back with a *poof* of smoke and a cooling "hiss".
- **Sticker:** "Lava Monster" (Adventures page).

### Drinking the water

- Staying in water for 1 second makes the character **drink it**: head tilts down, *glug glug glug* bubbles, cheeks puff up. Then a **big burp** with a burp bubble that floats up and pops.
- **Zoomies:** after drinking you get **zoomies** for 6 seconds: walk and dig 1.5× faster, with a splashy blue trail.
- You can drink again after 3 seconds out of the water.
- **Sticker:** "Big Drink" (Adventures page).
- **More water:** pools also appear in the Dino Bone Beds (oases) and the Brick Caverns. The camp pond decoration can be drunk from too.

- **7. Deep World:** amber, brick, star shard, pterodactyl, toy robot, alien, ember wisp, dino skeleton, meteorite, Heart of the World, spring block (11).

## 10. Save format v4

- The bank gains `amber`, `brick`, `star` and `heart`.
- Plots grow to 9.
- `records.layers: [layer ids reached]`, `records.moonTrips`.
- Migration fills the new keys; nothing is lost.

## 11. HUD changes

- **The ore strips** (mine panels and camp bank) show the 5 original ores plus each deep ore **once you've found it**, so the panels only grow as you discover things. The panels stretch to fit.
- **The Heart** shows next to the bank once you have one.

## 12. Architecture

- **Pure modules with tests:**
  - `world/worldgen.js`: layer table driven, layers 5–8, the Heart, oases, bounce blocks, meteorites, dino eggs, skeleton decorations; a `moon` preset;
  - `game/player.js`: low gravity; bounce blocks; the lava monster and zoomies as speed/dig multipliers passed in;
  - `game/powerups.js` (new): lava monster and drinking/zoomies timers and rules;
  - `game/economy.js` / `game/goals.js`: new tiers, ores and buildings;
  - `game/pets.js`: dino kinds;
  - `game/stickers.js`: new pages;
  - `save/save.js`: v4.
- **Scenes:**
  - `MineScene` takes `{ world: 'earth' | 'moon' }`;
  - new `LaunchScene` (the rocket cutscene);
  - the camp gets the rocket and the elevator picker;
  - the HUD shows discovered ores only.

## 13. Testing

- **Unit tests:** every new rule. Layers and their ores; hardness per tool tier; the Heart; oases; low gravity; bounce; the lava-monster timer and lava immunity; the drink timer, zoomies and cooldown; the new costs; the elevator's reachable layers; dino pets; the moon preset; v3 → v4 migration; sticker pages (69).
- **Browser:** each layer, the lava monster, drinking, dino pets, the rocket launch and the Moon, screenshotted and sent to the owner.
- **Balance:** the progression bot runs over many trips. The target: the Moon should take several sessions, not one.
- **Scoring** until ≥ 8.5.
