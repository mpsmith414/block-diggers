# M9 — Pets — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Eggs carried home hatch into pets that come on every trip and help: the mole sniffs out ore, the glow-bug lights the way, the bat buddy fetches loose ore. At home, the pets wander the camp.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-expansion-design.md` §4.

## Tasks
1. **`game/pets.js`** (pure):
   - `PET_KINDS`;
   - `hatch(state, kind, rng)` → `{ state, pet: kind|null, gold: n }`: a golden egg, or an egg of a pet you already have, gives 6 gold;
   - `sniff(grid, cx, cy, r)` → the nearest ore cell or null;
   - `follow(pos, target, dt, speed)` → the new position, easing and capped at `speed`;
   - `nearestPickup(pickups, x, y, r)`.

   **Tests:** a new pet is added; a duplicate or golden egg gives gold; sniff finds the nearest ore and ignores rock; follow never overshoots; `nearestPickup` respects r and pickup delay.
2. `attractPickups` takes a radius multiplier (the bat buddy doubles it). **Test:** a pickup beyond the normal radius is pulled in with ×2.
3. **Art:** `pet-mole`, `pet-glowbug`, `pet-batbuddy` (2 frames each), and `nest`.
4. **Mine:** `petsView`:
   - the mole trails player 1 and sniffs every 6 s (a trail of sparkles and a ping at the ore);
   - the glow-bug orbits player 1's head and adds light;
   - the bat buddy follows player 2 (or 1), and fetches pickups within 5 blocks and delivers them.
5. **Camp:**
   - a nest by the campfire;
   - after the summary and deposit, each carried egg wobbles, cracks and hatches, with confetti, the pet's sticker and a new pet (golden eggs burst into gold);
   - pets wander the camp.
6. **Verify** in the browser: hatch all three and screenshot each helping in the mine.
