# M25: The Sun (the finale) — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Sun as the last planet, with the grand finale. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-sun-finale-design.md`.

**Architecture:** The planet recipe again (tables, a generator on planetgen, two art files). Flares reuse the storm cycle with their own timings. The finale is a camp sequence triggered by the Sun's Heart and replayed from the Hall of Heroes. `sunHeart` in the save puts a mini-sun over Earth camp.

**Tech Stack:** Phaser 3.90, Vite, Vitest.

## Global Constraints

- Icons and sounds only: nothing a child needs to read.
- Gentle: on the Sun nothing can hurt you.
- Old saves load with nothing lost (v8 → v9).
- Block ids and tileset frames are only ever appended.
- The other planets play exactly as before.
