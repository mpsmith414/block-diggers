# M18: The Silly Update — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make the game funnier for a 6-year-old: creatures with personality, funny reactions, and joke finds, with a new "Silly" sticker page.

**Architecture:** Timers and rules go in a new pure module `game/silly.js` and in `world/worldgen.js` (ducks and cushions), with tests first. Scenes only play them out. Sounds are new WebAudio voices, kept quiet.

**Tech Stack:** Phaser 3.90, Vite, Vitest.

## Global Constraints

- Icons and sounds only: nothing to read.
- Nothing silly ever costs the player anything: no ore is lost, and no bonks.
- Sounds stay gentle and never loud (keep gain ≤ 0.15, like the other sfx).
- The repo is public: no family names.

## What's in it

**Creatures with personality**
- Each creature has its own voice now and then while it's on screen:
  - pterodactyl: caw;
  - bat: squeak;
  - toy robot: whirr;
  - alien: giggle, with a music note, when you're close (also earns a sticker);
  - ember wisp: crackle;
  - slime: blorp;
  - moon blob: boing.
- Squashed toy robots **fall over**: they tip, a spring pops out, and they fade.
- The pet T-rex **stomps** when it roars: a little screen shake and dust puffs.

**Funny reactions**
- **Sneeze:** digging lots of dusty blocks (dirt, sand) makes you sneeze now and then. You stop, lean back, then ACHOO with a dust cloud and a tiny hop backwards.
- **Dizzy:** landing after a long fall (6+ rows, not in water) gives you stars circling your head and a wobble for 1.5 s. You can still move.
- **Pet tricks:** press Y at camp (or in the mine when playing alone) and your pets do a trick: a flip, a spin or a hop, with hearts.

**Joke finds**
- **Rubber ducks** bob on some pools in the mine (up to 3). Touch one: SQUEAK-quack, and it hops out. Once found, a duck floats in your camp pond.
- **Smelly sock:** 1 chest in 5 also has a sock. It pops out with green stink lines and a "pee-yew" slide whistle.
- **Whoopee cushions** sit on cave floors (4 per mine). Step on one: PFFFBT! It flattens, then puffs back up.

**Stickers:** a new page, **Silly**, with 7 stickers (duck, sock, whoopee cushion, sneeze, dizzy, pet trick, giggle), making 76 stickers on 10 pages, plus trophy 9.

### Task 1: Rules (TDD)
- `game/silly.js`: `createSneeze(rng)`, `addDust(s, hardness, rng)` (true when it's time to sneeze; dust counts only for `soft` and `sand`; the threshold is 18–30 blocks); `createFall()`, `trackFall(f, p)` (returns `'dizzy'` on a landing after a drop of `SILLY.dizzyRows` or more rows; water resets it); `chestSock(rng)`; `cushionPressed(cushion, box, vy)`.
- `worldgen`: `ducks` (water cells with air above, up to 3) and `cushions` (safe floor cells from dirt to crystal, 4, not on eggs or chests).
- Stickers: 10 pages / 76; `trophy-9` decoration.

- `sfx`: `quack`, `peeyew`, `pffbt`, `achoo`, `dizzy`, `giggle`, `caw`, `squeak`, `whirr`, `blorp`, `crackle`, `trick`.
