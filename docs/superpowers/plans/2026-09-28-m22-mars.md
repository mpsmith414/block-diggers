# M22: Mars — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Mars as planet 2: a 5-layer mine with dust storms, geysers, old rovers and vaults, Mars Base with 4 buildings, the Boots, the Rover Bot, and the Saturn Rocket. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-28-mars-design.md`.

**Architecture:** Moon-only ternaries become planet recipes (`game/planets.js` and per-planet tables), so Mars is mostly data plus its generator (`world/mars.js`, sharing helpers with the Moon in `world/planetgen.js`) and its art. Storms are a pure step module (`game/storms.js`) drawn by `scenes/mine/stormView.js`.

**Tech Stack:** Phaser 3.90, Vite, Vitest.

## Global Constraints

- Icons and sounds only: nothing a child needs to read.
- Gentle hazards: bonks scatter ore and never end a trip; storms only push.
- Old saves must load, with nothing lost (v5 → v6).
- Block ids and tileset frames are only ever appended.
- The repo is public: no family names.
- The Earth and the Moon play exactly as before.

### Task 2: Storms and wind (TDD)
**Files:** `src/game/storms.js` (new), `src/game/player.js`, tests.
- `STORM` in tuning: `{ first: [20, 30], calm: [40, 70], warn: 3, blow: 8, push: 38 }`.
- `createStorm(rng)` returns `{ phase: 'calm', t, dir: 0 }`.
- `stepStorm(s, dt, rng)` returns the events `['warn' | 'start' | 'end']` and cycles calm → warn → blow → calm.
- `windOf(s)` is `dir * STORM.push` while blowing, else 0.
- `stepPlayer(..., { windX })` adds `windX` to `vx`, but not when climbing, knocked back or in water.
- Tests: the cycle order and timings, the direction is ±1, `windOf` in each phase, the player drifts with the wind when idle, walking against the wind is slower but still moves, and there's no drift on a ladder.
