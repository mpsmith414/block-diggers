# Block Diggers — Milestone 4: Hazards — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Gentle danger. Slimes hop (squash them from above), gravel falls when undermined, lava and bats bonk. A bonk knocks you back and scatters up to 3 ores that anyone can pick up for 10 s. Nothing else is ever lost.

**Architecture:** Pure `src/game/hazards.js` holds the entity step functions, the touch rules, gravel falling and lava escape. `loot.js` gains `scatterOres`, and `player.js` gains `knockback`. `MineScene` spawns enemies off-screen, steps them, and turns touches into bonks and squashes. Enemy sprites are drawn in `textures.js`.

**Spec:** main spec §1 "Hazards and the bonk".

## Global Constraints

- Bonk: knock back, scatter ≤ 3 ores (ttl 10 s, pickup delay 0.6 s), 1.5 s invulnerable. Bubbled players can't be bonked.
- Slimes in layers 1–2 (rows 1–95), at most 6. Bats in layer 3 (rows 96–148), at most 4. Both spawn only off-screen, near the players.
- Landing on a slime from above squashes it (poof, the player bounces). Touching it from the side is a bonk.
- Gravel (layer 2+): when the block under it is mined it shakes for 0.5 s, then falls, bonks any player it hits, and lands as a block. Gravel stacked above follows.
- Lava is static. Touching it is a bonk, and the player pops up to the nearest safe open cell above.

### Task 1: scatter + knockback

- `scatterOres(pack, rng, max = 3)` → ores removed (random picks weighted by count). Tests: never more than max, never more than the pack holds, the pack count drops to match, an empty pack gives [].
- `knockback(p, dir)`: sets `p.knock = { vx, t }` and an upward `vy`, and clears mining; `stepPlayer` uses `knock.vx` instead of input while `t > 0`. Test: the player moves away from the hazard even while pushing toward it.

### Task 2: hazards.js

- `overlaps(a, b)` for boxes `{ x, y, w, h }`.
- Slime: `createSlime(x, y, dir)` → box 12×10; `stepSlime(s, grid, dt)`: gravity; hops every `SLIME.hopEvery` s toward `dir`; turns around at walls and at drops higher than 2 blocks. `slimeTouch(playerBox, playerVy, slime)` → `'squash' | 'bonk' | null` (squash when falling and the player's bottom is within the slime's top 6 px).
- Bat: `createBat(x, y, dir)` → box 10×8; `stepBat(b, grid, dt)`: flies horizontally at `BAT.speed` on a sine wave around `baseY`, turns around at solid cells.
- Gravel: `triggerGravel(fallers, grid, x, y)` starts a shake if `(x, y)` is gravel and isn't already shaking. `stepGravel(fallers, grid, dt)` → `{ fallers, landed: [{x, y}], freed: [{x, y}] }`: after the shake the cell becomes air (freed) and a falling box drops; it lands on the first solid cell (or chest) and becomes gravel again (landed). Stacked gravel above a freed cell starts shaking.
- `lavaEscape(grid, cx, cy)` → the nearest cell above that is open, not lava, and not over lava.
- `spawnSpot(grid, rng, { kind, near, avoid })` → a cell for `'slime'` (open, solid below, rows 1–95) or `'bat'` (open, rows 96–148), within 12 rows / 20 columns of `near`, and outside the `avoid` rect. `null` if none found in 30 tries.

**Tests** cover each rule above: a slime lands, hops toward `dir`, and turns at a wall; squash vs bonk; a bat reverses at a wall and stays near `baseY`; gravel shakes 0.5 s, falls, lands, and chains; lava escape; spawn spots obey their layer and avoid rect.

### Task 3: scene + art

- Art: slime (2 frames), bat (2 frames), block frames on the `tiles` texture so falling gravel can be a sprite.
- `MineScene`: spawner (every 1.5 s, caps), despawn far-away enemies, step entities, bonk and squash with effects, gravel triggers after every mined cell, lava check, a blinking sprite while invulnerable, and scattered ore that blinks before it vanishes.
